### Production Plan for Graphic Designer Content

#### 1. Full Shot List with Shot Number, Type, Lens, and Lighting Setup

**Shot List:**
- **Shot 1:** Introduction (Talking Head)
    - **Type:** Wide shot of the speaker facing camera.
    - **Lens:** 35mm
    - **Lighting Setup:** Softbox on left side for fill light, softbox on right side for key light.
    
- **Shot 2:** Logo Creation Demonstration
    - **Type:** Over-the-shoulder view of computer screen with graphic designer working.
    - **Lens:** 50mm
    - **Lighting Setup:** Softbox above the monitor to illuminate the screen and work area.

- **Shot 3:** Design Tips & Tricks (Talking Head)
    - **Type:** Medium shot of speaker facing camera.
    - **Lens:** 85mm
    - **Lighting Setup:** Key light from front left, fill light from right side with softbox diffusers.

- **Shot 4:** Behind-the-Scenes Content (B-Roll)
    - **Type:** Wide shots and close-ups of the graphic designer's workspace.
    - **Lens:** Various lenses for different compositions (16mm to 50mm).
    - **Lighting Setup:** Natural light supplemented with softbox overhead.

#### 2. Production Schedule from Arrival to Wrap

**Production Schedule:**
- **Arrival Time:** 9:00 AM
- **Setup and Rehearsal:** 9:30 AM - 10:00 AM
    - Set up equipment, lighting, and camera.
    - Brief the client on the day's schedule and shot list.
    
- **Shooting Session:**
    - Introduction (Shot 1): 10:00 AM - 10:15 AM
    - Logo Creation Demonstration (Shot 2): 10:15 AM - 11:30 AM
    - Design Tips & Tricks (Shot 3): 11:45 AM - 12:30 PM
    - Behind-the-Scenes Content (B-Roll) (Shot 4): 12:30 PM - 1:30 PM

- **Lunch Break:** 1:30 PM - 2:00 PM
    
- **Post-Shooting Cleanup and Wrap-Up:** 2:00 PM - 2:30 PM
    - Pack up equipment.
    - Review footage with client.
