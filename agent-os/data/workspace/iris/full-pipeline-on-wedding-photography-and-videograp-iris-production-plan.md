### Wedding Photography and Videography Production Plan

#### 1. Shot List
- **Shot 01:** Opening wide shot of the wedding venue from a drone perspective (Drone, Wide Angle Lens)
- **Shot 02:** Bride getting ready in her room with close-ups on makeup application and hair styling (Camera, Close-up Lens, Softbox Lighting)
- **Shot 03:** Groom preparing in his suite with details of attire and accessories (Camera, Medium Telephoto Lens, Key Light Setup)
- **Shot 04:** Bridal party getting ready with candid moments and interactions (Camera, Wide Angle Lens, Natural Light)
- **Shot 05:** Bride walking down the aisle from various angles including emotional reactions (Camera, Medium Telephoto Lens, Softbox Lighting)
- **Shot 06:** Ceremony highlights focusing on vows exchange and rings being exchanged (Camera, Close-up Lens, Fill Light Setup)
- **Shot 07:** Emotional moments between bride and groom during ceremony (Camera, Wide Angle Lens, Natural Light)
- **Shot 08:** Bride and groom leaving the venue with family and friends cheering (Drone, Wide Angle Lens)
- **Shot 09:** Reception setup shots including decor details and table arrangements (Camera, Medium Telephoto Lens, Softbox Lighting)
- **Shot 10:** First dance between bride and groom from different angles (Camera, Close-up Lens, Key Light Setup)
- **Shot 11:** Cutting cake ceremony with candid moments of guests enjoying themselves (Camera, Wide Angle Lens, Natural Light)
- **Shot 12:** Bride throwing bouquet to bridesmaids in slow motion (Camera, Medium Telephoto Lens, Softbox Lighting)
- **Shot 13:** Groom smashing the glass underfoot for a traditional Jewish wedding ceremony (Camera, Close-up Lens, Key Light Setup)
- **Shot 14:** Final group shot of all guests and family members with the couple in front (Drone, Wide Angle Lens)

#### 3. Equipment List
- Cameras: Canon EOS R5, Nikon D850
- Lenses: 24mm f/1.4, 50mm f/1.2, 70-200mm f/2.8
- Lighting: Softbox Kit, Key Light Setup
- Drone: DJI Mavic Pro
- Audio: Rode NTG3 Microphone

#### 4. Client Prep Instructions
- **Bride:** Wear comfortable clothing for getting ready shots and bring your wedding dress to the room.
- **Groom:** Have attire and accessories prepared in a separate space for preparation shots.
- **Bridal Party:** Arrive early to allow time for candid moments and interactions.
- **Venue Staff:** Ensure that all necessary areas are set up before scheduled shooting times.

#### 5. Post-Production Notes
- **Color Grade:** Warm, moody tones with airy highlights for a romantic feel.
- **Edit Style:** Clean, fast-paced edits with clear text overlays providing practical tips and emotional moments.
- **Music Suggestions:** Soft background music like "Wedding Day" by Kevin MacLeod or custom wedding playlist.
- **Delivery Format:** 1080p HD video files delivered via Dropbox within one week post-event.

#### 6. OTG Team Assignments
- **Jack Riggs:** Camera and drone operations, capturing all key moments from various angles.
- **Swope or Teja:** Editing the footage to create a cohesive narrative with practical tips and emotional highlights.
