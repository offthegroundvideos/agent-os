### Production Plan for Dog Training Content

#### 1. Full Shot List with Shot Number, Type, Lens, and Lighting Setup

**Shot 1: Introduction**
- **Type:** Talking Head (Close-up)
- **Lens:** 50mm
- **Lighting:** Softbox on the left side of the camera
- **Notes:** Jack will introduce himself and briefly explain the topic.

**Shot 2: Problem Statement**
- **Type:** Talking Head (Medium Shot)
- **Lens:** 35mm
- **Lighting:** Natural light from a window
- **Notes:** Explain common dog behavior problems like excessive barking or biting.

**Shot 3: Solution Introduction**
- **Type:** Talking Head (Close-up)
- **Lens:** 85mm
- **Lighting:** Softbox on the right side of the camera
- **Notes:** Introduce the training program and its benefits.

**Shot 4: Step-by-Step Training Guide - Part 1**
- **Type:** Demonstration with Dog (Close-up)
- **Lens:** 24mm
- **Lighting:** Ring light around the camera
- **Notes:** Show how to train a dog to sit using positive reinforcement techniques.

**Shot 5: Step-by-Step Training Guide - Part 2**
- **Type:** Demonstration with Dog (Medium Shot)
- **Lens:** 35mm
- **Lighting:** Natural light from the window
- **Notes:** Show how to train a dog to stay using consistent commands and rewards.

**Shot 6: Before-and-After Transformation - Part 1**
- **Type:** B-Roll Footage (Wide Shot)
- **Lens:** 24mm
- **Lighting:** Natural light from the window
- **Notes:** Show clips of a dog with behavior issues before training.

**Shot 7: Before-and-After Transformation - Part 2**
- **Type:** B-Roll Footage (Medium Shot)
- **Lens:** 50mm
- **Lighting:** Softbox on the left side of the camera
- **Notes:** Show clips of a dog after successful training.

**Shot 8: Conclusion and CTA**
- **Type:** Talking Head (Close-up)
- **Lens:** 85mm
- **Lighting:** Ring light around the camera
- **Notes:** Recap the benefits of the training program and encourage viewers to book a consultation.

#### 2. Production Schedule from Arrival to Wrap

**Arrival:**
- 9:00 AM - Set up equipment, lighting, and background.

**Production:**

- 10:45 AM - Step-by-step training guide part 1

#### 3. Equipment List
- **Cameras:** Canon EOS R6 (x2)
- **Lenses:** 
  - 24mm f/1.8
  - 35mm f/1.8
  - 50mm f/1.8
  - 85mm f/1.8
- **Lights:**
  - Softbox (x2)
  - Ring light
  - Natural light from window
- **Drone:** DJI Mavic Air 2
- **Audio:**
  - Rode NTG4+ Microphone
  - Audio interface

#### 4. Client Prep Instructions
- Wear comfortable clothing and shoes suitable for training sessions.
- Bring your dog to the shooting location.
- Be ready to discuss common behavior issues and how they affect your daily life.

#### 5. Post-Production Notes
**Color Grade:**
- Warm tone with a slight moody feel, enhancing the natural skin tones and lighting conditions.
  
**Edit Style:**
- Fast-paced editing to keep viewers engaged.
- Use text overlays for key points and step-by-step instructions.

**Music Suggestions:**
- Upbeat background music (80 BPM)
- Calming background music for before-and-after transformation scenes

#### 6. OTG Team Assignments
- **Jack:** Camera, drone footage, and overall production direction.
- **Swope or Teja:** Editing the final video.
