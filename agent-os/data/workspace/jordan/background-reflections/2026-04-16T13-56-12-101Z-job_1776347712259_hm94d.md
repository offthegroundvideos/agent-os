### Growth Strategy for OTG Icon - Jack Riggs

#### 1. WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?
**Target Customers:**
- Small local businesses (e.g., real estate agents, restaurants)
- Wedding couples
- Corporate clients
- Influencers
- E-commerce brands
- Nonprofits
- Event organizers
- Musicians
- Artists
- Tourism boards
- Yacht and marina owners
- Travel brands
- Media companies needing stock footage

**Where They Spend Time Online:**
- **Instagram:** Short-form video content (Reels, Stories)
- **TikTok:** Fast-paced tutorials, direct camera shots
- **LinkedIn:** Professional networking and thought leadership posts
- **YouTube:** Educational content and detailed case studies
- **Facebook Groups:** Engaging with photography communities

#### 2. WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?
**Primary Offer:**
- **Photography Services Retainer:** A monthly retainer for ongoing photography services tailored to specific client needs (e.g., wedding planning, real estate listings).

#### 3. HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?
**Low-Cost Acquisition Channels:**
- **Social Media Engagement:** Utilize Instagram Reels and TikTok duets/challenges to increase visibility.
- **LinkedIn Thought Leadership:** Share detailed case studies and educational content on LinkedIn.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Collaborate with other creators for cross-promotion.

#### 4. WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?
**Unique Selling Points:**
- **Personalized Service:** Tailored photography services based on client needs.
- **High-Quality Content:** Professional-grade visuals and detailed case studies.
- **Educational Value:** Actionable insights and step-by-step tutorials for businesses.

#### 5. WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?
**Optimal Moments:**
- After posting a compelling Reel or TikTok video showcasing photography tips.
- Directly after sharing a LinkedIn post with a detailed case study.
- Upon engagement in Facebook groups with valuable content contributions.

### Growth Strategy Deliverables

#### 1. Primary Offer
- **Photography Services Retainer:** Monthly retainer for ongoing photography services tailored to client needs (e.g., wedding planning, real estate listings).

#### 2. 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan with Specific Milestones
**30-Day Goals:**
- Post 15 high-quality short-form videos on Instagram and TikTok.
- Engage in at least 5 LinkedIn thought leadership posts.
- Collaborate with 3 other creators on YouTube Shorts.

**60-Day Goals:**
- Increase social media engagement by 25%.
- Generate 10 new leads through retainer offers.
- Expand content reach to additional platforms like Facebook and Pinterest.

**90-Day Goals:**
- Secure at least 3 monthly retainer clients.
- Launch a referral program for existing clients.
- Optimize funnel conversion rates based on initial performance data.

#### 3. Complete Funnel - Content to Closed Deal
1. **Social Content:** Engaging short-form videos on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts.
2. **Trigger to ManyChat:** Direct messaging through thought leadership posts or duet challenges.
3. **Landing Page:** Customized landing page for retainer offers with detailed services and pricing.
4. **Lead Magnet Delivery:** Free photography tips guide delivered via email after form submission.
5. **VSL/Demo:** Video showcasing OTG's unique value proposition and success stories.
6. **Booking Call:** Follow-up call to discuss client needs and tailor the offer.
7. **Proposal:** Customized proposal outlining services, pricing, and timeline.
8. **Contract Signing:** Formal contract agreement for retainer services.
9. **Delivery:** Ongoing photography services provided according to the agreed schedule.
10. **Review Request:** Client satisfaction survey sent after service delivery.
11. **Referral Request:** Incentivized referral program offered post-delivery.

#### 4. ManyChat Setup Using Trigger Words
**Trigger Word:**
- `book_wedding`, `real_estate`, `commercial_photos`, `travel_photography`

**ManyChat Flow:**
- **Welcome Message:** "Hi there! Thanks for reaching out about our photography services."
- **Form Submission:** Collect basic contact information and specific needs.
- **Service Options:** Provide options for wedding, real estate, commercial, or travel photography retainer packages.
- **Follow-Up Call Setup:** Schedule a call to discuss the client's requirements in detail.

#### 5. 3 Monetization Strategies
1. **Photography Retainers:** Monthly retainer services tailored to clients' specific needs.
2. **Stock Footage Licensing:** Monetize OTG's extensive stock footage library through subscription and per-clip licensing models.
3. **Workshops & Courses:** Offer in-depth photography workshops and courses for professional development.

#### 6. Weekly KPIs
1. **Social Media Engagement Rate:**
   - Instagram/TikTok: Comments, likes, shares per post.
2. **Lead Generation Rate:**
   - Number of leads generated from each platform weekly.
3. **Conversion Rate:**
   - Percentage of leads converting to paying clients.

#### 7. Objection Map
| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| Too expensive | "Our retainer services offer significant value and long-term benefits, tailored specifically to your needs." |
| No time for training | "We provide detailed guides and support throughout the process to ensure a smooth experience." |
| Lack of trust in quality | "View our portfolio and testimonials from satisfied clients to see our commitment to excellence." |

#### 8. What NOT to Do
- **Avoid vague or generic content:** Ensure each video teaches one specific idea.
- **Do not over-edit:** Keep the visual style clean but professional.
- **No jargon in first sentence:** Start value immediately after the hook.

#### 9. Testing Plan
1. **Post 50 Videos Across Platforms:**
   - Monitor engagement rates and identify top-performing hooks and formats.
2. **Analyze Data for Winners:**
   - Use performance metrics to determine which videos generate the most leads.
3. **Double Down on Successful Hooks:**
   - Repurpose winning content into multiple variations to maximize reach.
