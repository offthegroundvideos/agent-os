### Growth Strategy for Jack Riggs: Photography Business in Marin County

#### 1. WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?
**Target Customer:** Models aged 18-30, primarily women who are active in the photography community in Marin County.
**Online Presence:** Instagram (Reels), TikTok, Facebook, Threads.

#### 2. WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?
**Primary Offer:** Behind-the-scenes prep tips and quick styling hacks for photoshoots via social media content.

#### 3. HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?
**Low-Cost Acquisition Channels:**
- Leverage Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook, and Threads to publish engaging behind-the-scenes content.
- Participate in online communities and forums where models gather (e.g., Model Mayhem).
- Network with other photographers and modeling agencies to build referrals.

#### 4. WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?
**Unique Selling Points:**
- Personalized storytelling techniques that set photoshoots apart from competitors.
- Behind-the-scenes content that provides value upfront, building trust before the sale.
- Clear and concise communication through social media platforms.

#### 5. WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?
**Right Moment:** After a model has engaged with multiple pieces of behind-the-scenes content, shown interest via comments or DMs, and seen positive testimonials from previous clients.

### Growth Strategy Deliverables

1. **Primary Offer:**
   - Behind-the-Scenes Prep Tips & Quick Styling Hacks for Photoshoots.
   
2. **30/60/90 Day Growth Plan with Specific Milestones:**

   **Week 1 (Day 1-7):** Publish initial behind-the-scenes prep tips and styling hacks on Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook, and Threads.
   
   **Week 4:** Post client testimonials and case studies to build social proof.

   **Month 2 (Days 30-60):** Expand content types to include more detailed tutorials and styling guides. Begin targeted ads on Instagram and TikTok to reach a wider audience.

   **Month 3 (Days 60-90):** Launch referral program for models who bring in new clients. Increase frequency of client testimonials and success stories.

3. **Complete Funnel:**
   - Social Content → Comment/DM Trigger → ManyChat → Landing Page → Lead Magnet Delivered → VSL/Demo to Booking Call → Proposal to Contract → Delivery Review Request → Referral Request.
   
4. **ManyChat Setup using Trigger Words (Sam specified):**
   - Trigger Word: "Shine"
   - Trigger Action: When a user comments or DMs the word "shine," they are prompted with a ManyChat bot that delivers lead magnets and directs them to booking calls.

5. **3 Monetization Strategies:**
   - Monthly Retainer for Regular Photoshoot Sessions
   - One-Time Package Deals for Special Events (e.g., Fashion Shows)
   - Stock Photography Licensing

6. **Weekly KPIs:**
   - Number of new followers gained per week.
   - Engagement rate on posted content (likes, comments, shares).
   - Conversion rate from social media to booking calls.

7. **Objection Map:**
   - **Objection:** "I'm not sure if I need a photoshoot."
     - **Answer:** Emphasize the value of high-quality images for career advancement and personal branding.
   - **Objection:** "Your prices are higher than other photographers in Marin County."
     - **Answer:** Highlight unique selling points such as personalized storytelling, behind-the-scenes content, and consistent social media presence.

8. **What NOT to Do:**
   - Avoid posting vague or overly promotional content that doesn't provide immediate value.
   - Don’t overcomplicate the funnel with too many steps before asking for a booking call.
   
9. **Testing Plan:**
   - Post 50-100 videos across platforms within the first month, focusing on different hooks and formats (e.g., visual, written, verbal).
   - Identify top-performing content based on engagement rates and conversion metrics.
   - Double down on successful hooks and formats by producing more similar content.
