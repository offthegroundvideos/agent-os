1. **WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?**
   - Exact Target Customer: Couples planning their weddings, especially those who prioritize capturing memories through high-quality videography.
   - Online Presence:
     - Instagram (primary): Engage with wedding-related hashtags and communities like #WeddingVideography, #CaptureEveryMoment, #MarriageMatters.
     - TikTok: Share behind-the-scenes content, tips, and engaging short-form videos using trending challenges or music.
     - LinkedIn: For professional networking and business-to-business clients interested in corporate branding and event coverage.

2. **WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?**
   - Offer: A retainer package for wedding videography services with a bonus honeymoon destination video shoot included, priced competitively at $1500-$3997 per month.
   - This offers couples comprehensive coverage of their special day and an additional value-added service to capture the honeymoon memories.

3. **HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?**
   - Leverage organic social media growth:
     - Use Instagram Reels and TikTok Shorts with engaging visual, written, and verbal hooks.
     - Collaborate with wedding influencers for shoutouts and product placements.
     - Engage in community building by participating in wedding forums and local event planning groups.

4. **WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?**
   - Unique Selling Points:
     - Personalized service tailored to each couple's unique needs, ensuring their story is told authentically.
     - Cutting-edge technology like drone footage for breathtaking aerial shots and high-definition videography.
     - Comprehensive packages that include both wedding day coverage and honeymoon destination videos.

5. **WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?**
   - Timing: After sharing a series of engaging posts, direct camera addresses inviting viewers to engage, and showcasing behind-the-scenes content, use ManyChat triggers with specific keywords (e.g., "book", "reserve") to capture leads.
   - Offer a compelling lead magnet such as an e-book on top wedding videography tips or a free consultation call.

### 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan

**30-Day Goal:**
- Establish OTG Icon’s presence in the wedding videography niche through consistent posting (50 videos).
- Build engagement by interacting with target audience and influencers.
- Test various visual, written, and verbal hooks to identify top performers.

**60-Day Goal:**
- Double daily video production to 100 posts, focusing on successful formats identified during testing phase.
- Integrate ManyChat triggers for lead capture and nurture sequences.
- Begin collaborating with wedding planners and venues for cross-promotional opportunities.

**90-Day Goal:**
- Achieve a consistent follower growth rate of at least 5% per month.
- Launch retainer packages with honeymoon destination video shoots as an added value proposition.
- Expand reach by targeting additional platforms like YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels.
