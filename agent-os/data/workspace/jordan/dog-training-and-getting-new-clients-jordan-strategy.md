### Growth Strategy for OTG Icon - Dog Training Niche

#### 1. Who is the Exact Target Customer and Where Do They Spend Time Online?
- **Target Audience:** Pet owners aged 25-45, specifically those actively seeking solutions to dog training issues.
- **Platform Preference:** Instagram and TikTok are primary platforms for engagement.

#### 2. What is the One Offer That Converts Them Fastest?
- **Offer:** A personalized consultation or a package of step-by-step instructional videos tailored to specific dog training challenges (e.g., sit, stay, no biting).

#### 3. How Do We Get in Front of Them at Lowest Cost?
- **Strategy:** Leverage organic growth tactics such as duet challenges, step-by-step guides, and real-life scenario demonstrations.
- **Channels:**
  - Instagram Reels
  - TikTok Duet Challenges

#### 4. Why Will They Choose OTG Over Competitors?
- **Unique Selling Points (USPs):** High-quality visual content, personalized approach, and practical solutions that go beyond basic training tips.

#### 5. When is the Right Moment to Ask for the Sale?
- **Trigger:** After viewers have engaged with multiple videos, seen consistent value, and are ready to take action.
- **CTA Timing:**
  - Direct them to DM 'command' (as per Sam's scripts) after demonstrating a successful training technique.

### 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan

#### 30-Day Goal
- Publish at least 2 videos per week on Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- Engage with the community through duet challenges and real-life scenario demonstrations.
- Build initial engagement metrics (views, likes, comments).

#### 60-Day Goal
- Increase video publication frequency to 4 videos per week.
- Introduce a series of step-by-step instructional videos.
- Start seeing higher engagement rates and growing follower count.

#### 90-Day Goal
- Publish comprehensive guides on LinkedIn for broader reach.
- Begin offering personalized consultations based on viewer requests.
- Establish OTG as a trusted authority in the dog training niche.

### Complete Funnel - Content to Closed Deal

1. **Social Content**
   - Step-by-step instructional videos (Instagram Reels, TikTok)
   - Duet challenges and real-life scenario demonstrations
2. **Trigger to ManyChat**
   - Viewers DM 'command' for more information.
3. **Landing Page**
   - Detailed page explaining the benefits of personalized consultations.
4. **Lead Magnet Delivered**
   - Freebie: A 5-step guide to basic dog training techniques.
5. **VSL or Demo**
   - Watch video testimonials and case studies showcasing successful client outcomes.
6. **Booking Call**
   - Schedule a consultation call via ManyChat integration.
7. **Proposal**
   - Send personalized proposal outlining services, pricing, and next steps.
8. **Contract to Delivery**
   - Sign contract and begin service delivery (training sessions).
9. **Review Request**
   - Ask for reviews after successful completion of training packages.
10. **Referral Request**
    - Encourage clients to refer friends and family.

### ManyChat Setup

- **Trigger Word:** 'command'
  - When a user DMs 'command', they are directed to the personalized consultation landing page.

### Monetization Strategies
1. **Consultation Packages:**
   - Offer tiered packages for basic, intermediate, and advanced training needs.
2. **Premium Content Library:**
   - Monthly subscription model for exclusive access to step-by-step videos and expert advice.
3. **Stock Footage Sales:**
   - Monetize high-quality dog training footage through stock libraries.

### Weekly KPIs
- **Engagement Metrics:** Views, likes, comments, shares per video.
- **Follower Growth Rate:** Number of new followers gained each week.
- **Conversion Rate:** Percentage of DM interactions that lead to consultations or sales.

### What NOT to Do
1. **Avoid Over-Promotion:**
   - Focus on providing value and building trust before pushing hard sales.
2. **Skip Quality Content Production:**
   - Ensure videos are visually appealing and professionally produced.
3. **Ignore Community Engagement:**
   - Actively participate in community challenges and respond to viewer comments.

### Testing Plan
1. **Publish 50-100 Videos:**
   - Test various hooks, formats, and CTA triggers across different platforms.
2. **Identify Winners:**
   - Analyze which videos generate the most engagement and conversions.
3. **Double Down on Successes:**
   - Replicate successful patterns in future content while iterating on weaker elements.
