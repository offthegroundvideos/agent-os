1. **WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?**
   - Exact Target Customer: Local fireworks retailers, online sellers, event planners.
   - Online Presence: TikTok, Instagram Reels during peak seasons like July 4th and New Year's Eve.

2. **WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?**
   - Offer: High-quality visual content packages (DIY tutorials, safety tips, firework display showcases) tailored for social media engagement.

3. **HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?**
   - Leverage organic reach on TikTok and Instagram Reels.
   - Collaborate with micro-influencers in the niche to expand visibility.

4. **WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?**
   - Unique Selling Point: High-quality visual content that combines educational value, safety tips, and stunning firework displays.
   - Expertise: Specialized knowledge of firework business trends and seasonal marketing strategies.

5. **WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?**
   - Timing: Immediately after posting high-engagement videos on social media platforms.
   - Trigger: Use ManyChat automation to prompt interested viewers with a lead magnet after engaging with content.

### 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan

**30-Day Goal:** 
- Publish at least one video per day during peak season (July and August).
- Engage micro-influencers for cross-promotion.
- Build initial ManyChat automation sequences.

**60-Day Goal:**
- Analyze performance data from the first month to identify top-performing content formats and hooks.
- Expand reach by collaborating with more influencers and leveraging user-generated content.
- Optimize funnel conversion rates based on engagement metrics.

**90-Day Goal:**
- Double down on successful content strategies identified in the analysis phase.
- Launch targeted ad campaigns on TikTok and Instagram Reels to increase organic reach.
- Introduce new monetization ideas such as premium content packages or stock footage sales.

### Complete Funnel - Content to Closed Deal

1. **Social Content (TikTok, Instagram Reels)**
   - Hook: Engaging visual and written hooks that grab attention immediately.
   - Value: Educational tutorials, safety tips, firework display showcases.
2. **ManyChat Trigger**
   - Action: Comment or DM on social media posts.
3. **Landing Page (OTG Icon Website)**
   - Offer: High-quality visual content packages for fireworks businesses.
4. **Lead Magnet Delivered (Email/Text)**
   - Freebie: DIY Firework Tutorial E-Book.
5. **VSL/Demo (Zoom Call Invitation)**
   - Next Step: Booking a call to discuss custom content solutions.
6. **Proposal and Contract**
   - Finalize deal with detailed proposal and contract.
7. **Delivery of Content Packages**
   - Provide high-quality visual content tailored for social media.
8. **Review Request**
   - Follow up post-delivery to gather feedback and testimonials.
9. **Referral Request**
   - Encourage clients to refer other fireworks businesses.

### ManyChat Setup Using Trigger Words

- **Trigger Word:** #OTGBookNow
  - Action: When a user comments or DMs with the trigger word, they are prompted to enter their email address.
  - Automation Flow:
    - Welcome message with freebie offer (DIY Firework Tutorial E-Book).
    - Follow-up messages directing them to landing page for content packages.

### 3 Monetization Strategies

1. **High-Quality Visual Content Packages**
   - Offer customized visual content packages that include tutorials, safety tips, and firework display showcases.
2. **Stock Footage Library**
   - Sell access to a curated stock footage library featuring stunning firework displays.
3. **Customized Ad Campaigns**
   - Provide targeted ad campaign services on TikTok and Instagram Reels for fireworks businesses.

### Weekly KPIs

- Engagement Rate (likes, comments, shares per post)
- Follower Growth Rate
- ManyChat Trigger Engagement Rate
- Conversion Rates from Social Media to Landing Page
- Sales Revenue from Content Packages

### What NOT to Do

1. **Avoid posting generic content without a clear hook or value proposition.**
2. **Do not ignore engagement metrics and fail to adjust strategies accordingly.**
3. **Avoid over-relying on paid ads before establishing organic reach and credibility.**

### Testing Plan

- Post 50-100 videos in the first month, focusing on different hooks, formats, and content angles.
- Analyze performance data (engagement rates, conversion rates) to identify top-performing elements.
- Double down on successful strategies by creating more content in those formats/hooks.
