#### 1. WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?
**Target Customer:** Models in Marin County, CA who are actively seeking to improve their portfolios with high-quality photoshoots.
**Online Presence:**
- **Instagram:** Engaging with hashtags like #ModelLife, #PhotoshootReady, and following local modeling communities.
- **TikTok:** Participating in trending challenges and duets that showcase quick transformation tips.

#### 2. WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?
**Primary Offer:** Book a photoshoot session with OTG Icon to enhance your portfolio and stand out from the competition.

#### 3. HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?
**Low-Cost Acquisition:**
- **Social Media Challenges/Duets:** Engage models through TikTok challenges that highlight quick transformation tips.
- **Hashtag Campaigns:** Leverage Instagram hashtags to reach models seeking professional photoshoot opportunities.

#### 4. WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?
**Unique Value Propositions:**
- Custom B-roll footage and stronger first-frame hooks tailored specifically for models in Marin County, CA.
- High-resolution images with dynamic poses against clean backgrounds that emphasize texture and detail.
- Professional guidance on quick transformation tips to elevate their portfolio.

#### 5. WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?
**Optimal Timing:**
- After showcasing quick transformation tips through TikTok challenges or Instagram Reels.
- When models are engaged with content highlighting professional photoshoot benefits and custom B-roll footage.

### Growth Strategy Components

1. **Primary Offer:** Book a photoshoot session
2. **30/60/90 Day Growth Plan:**
   - **Week 1 (Day 1):** Launch TikTok challenge for quick transformation tips.
   - **Week 4 (Day 28):** Host Instagram Reel series showcasing professional photoshoot benefits and custom B-roll footage.
   - **Month 3 (Day 90):** Introduce referral program to encourage existing clients to bring in new models.

3. **Complete Funnel:**
   - **Content:** TikTok challenges, Instagram Reels
   - **Trigger:** Comment or DM engagement with "BOOK"
   - **Landing Page:** Detailed page explaining the photoshoot offer and benefits.
   - **Lead Magnet:** Downloadable guide on quick transformation tips.
   - **VSL/Demo:** Video showcasing past successful photoshoots and client testimonials.
   - **Booking Call:** Personalized consultation to discuss specific needs and vision for the shoot.
   - **Contract:** Formal agreement outlining terms, pricing, and deliverables.
   - **Delivery:** High-quality photos delivered in a professional portfolio format.
   - **Review Request:** Post-shoot feedback form to gather testimonials and reviews.

4. **ManyChat Setup:**
   - **Trigger Word:** "BOOK"
   - **Flow:** Engage users who comment or DM with the trigger word, direct them to the landing page for booking information, and provide a link to download the lead magnet guide.

5. **Monetization Strategies:**
   - **Retainer Plans:** Monthly retainer plans offering regular photoshoot sessions.
   - **Stock Footage Licensing:** Monetize custom B-roll footage through stock licensing platforms.
   - **Referral Program:** Incentivize existing clients to refer new models with discounts or free services.

6. **Weekly KPIs:**
   - Engagement rate on TikTok challenges and Instagram Reels
   - Conversion rates from "BOOK" triggers to booking consultations
   - Number of referral leads generated

7. **Objection Map:**
   - **High Cost:** Emphasize the value-added services like custom B-roll footage and professional guidance.
   - **Lack of Time:** Offer flexible scheduling options and quick turnaround times for photoshoots.

8. **What NOT to Do:**
   - Avoid generic content that does not resonate with models in Marin County, CA.
   - Refrain from overpromising features or services beyond what is offered.

9. **Testing Plan:**
   - Post 50-100 videos across TikTok and Instagram using various hooks and formats identified by the Viral Filter Agent.
   - Identify top-performing content based on engagement, conversion rates, and follower growth.
   - Double down on successful hooks and formats to scale reach and conversions.
