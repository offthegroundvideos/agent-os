### Growth Strategy for Michelle Elmore - Street Photography Niche

#### 1. WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?
- **Target Audience:** Art lovers, photography nerds aged 18-45.
- **Online Platforms:** Instagram (Reels, Stories), TikTok (Short videos with trending sounds, Duet opportunities).

#### 2. WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?
- **Primary Offer:** "Buy Michelle Elmore's Street Photography Book."

#### 3. HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?
- Use Instagram Reels and Stories for consistent engagement.
- Leverage TikTok duet opportunities to reach a broader audience.
- Engage with local photography clubs, art galleries in Marin County, and online communities.

#### 4. WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?
- **Unique Selling Points:** Personalized storytelling workshops and custom photo book design services.
- **Engagement Strategy:** Behind-the-scenes content and interactive challenges to build community engagement.
- **Expertise:** Michelle Elmore's expertise in street photography, combined with OTG Icon’s creative production capabilities.

#### 5. WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?
- After engaging followers through photo challenges and sharing behind-the-scenes content.
- Use Instagram Stories and Reels to introduce the book offer directly after building engagement.

### Growth Strategy Deliverables

1. **Primary Offer:**
   - "Buy Michelle Elmore's Street Photography Book."

2. **30/60/90 Day Growth Plan:**
   - **30 Days:** Build initial community through photo challenges and behind-the-scenes content.
     - Milestone 1: Engage with at least 5 local photography clubs.
     - Milestone 2: Post 20 Reels on Instagram and 15 TikTok videos.
   - **60 Days:** Introduce storytelling workshops and custom book design services.
     - Milestone 3: Host 3 live Q&A sessions on Instagram Live.
     - Milestone 4: Offer a free e-book to those who sign up for the workshop series.
   - **90 Days:** Ramp up sales efforts through targeted content and direct calls-to-action.
     - Milestone 5: Launch a limited-time offer campaign with discounts for book purchases.

3. **Complete Funnel:**
   - **Social Content →** Comment or DM Trigger → ManyChat → Landing Page → Lead Magnet (e-book) → VSL/Demo → Booking Call → Proposal → Contract → Delivery → Review Request → Referral Request → Repeat.

4. **ManyChat Setup:**
   - **Trigger Words:** "join the challenge," "storytelling tips"
   - **Flow Example:**
     - User triggers ManyChat through a comment or DM.
     - Welcome message with CTAs to sign up for workshops and free e-book.
     - Direct them to landing page.

5. **3 Monetization Strategies:**
   - **Book Sales:** Offer discounts on books purchased during limited-time campaigns.
   - **Workshops and Training Sessions:** Charge a fee for personalized storytelling workshops.
   - **Custom Photo Book Design Services:** Provide premium design services for an additional cost.

6. **Weekly KPIs:**
   - Engagement Rate (likes, comments, shares)
   - New Followers
   - Click-Through Rates to Landing Page
   - Lead Magnet Downloads

7. **Objection Map:**
   - **Objection:** "I don't have time for photo challenges."
     - Response: "The challenges are designed to be quick and fun. They're a great way to stay inspired between longer projects."
   - **Objection:** "I'm not sure if I need the book."
     - Response: "Our book offers unique insights into street photography that can elevate your skills, even if you already have experience."

8. **What NOT to Do:**
   - Don't post vague or disconnected content from the primary offer.
   - Avoid over-editing videos; keep them authentic and engaging.

9. **Testing Plan:**
   - Post 50-100 videos across Instagram Reels, Stories, and TikTok.
   - Identify top-performing hooks (visual, written, verbal) and formats.
   - Double down on successful strategies by posting more content in those styles.
