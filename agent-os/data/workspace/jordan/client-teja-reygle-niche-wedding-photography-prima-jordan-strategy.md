1. **WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?**
   - Exact Target Customer: Couples aged 25-35 who are newly engaged and planning their wedding in New York or Virginia.
   - Where They Spend Time Online: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn.

2. **WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?**
   - Primary Offer: Custom Wedding Photography Package ($2500-$8500) tailored to capture candid moments and behind-the-scenes preparations.

3. **HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?**
   - Use Instagram Reels, TikTok Short Videos, and Facebook Stories to showcase behind-the-scenes wedding preparation content.
   - Leverage hashtags like #WeddingPhotography, #JustEngaged, #NYCWeddings, and #VirginiaWeds.

4. **WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?**
   - OTG focuses on authentic storytelling and practical content that resonates with engaged couples looking for genuine moments.
   - Unique selling points include personalized service, behind-the-scenes access, and emotional image world creation.

5. **WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?**
   - After engaging potential clients through Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, and Facebook Stories by providing value with tips and behind-the-scenes content.
   - Trigger ManyChat conversations when users comment or interact with posts asking about booking a consultation call.

### Growth Strategy Deliverables

#### Primary Offer
- **Primary Offer:** Custom Wedding Photography Package ($2500-$8500) tailored to capture candid moments and behind-the-scenes preparations.

#### 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan
1. **30-Day Goal:**
   - Post 15 viral video scripts focusing on behind-the-scenes wedding prep.
   - Engage with followers through ManyChat by asking for their wedding visions and needs.
2. **60-Day Goal:**
   - Launch a series of Instagram Reels showcasing different wedding styles and stories.
   - Increase engagement rate by 30% compared to the first month.
3. **90-Day Goal:**
   - Secure at least 5 new bookings through social media campaigns.
   - Expand content reach to LinkedIn for business clients.

#### Complete Funnel
1. **Social Content (Instagram Reels, TikTok)**
2. **Comment or DM Trigger** 
   - ManyChat trigger word: "See the magic behind your wedding day."
3. **Landing Page (ManyChat Conversation)**
4. **Lead Magnet Delivered:** Customized Wedding Photography Consultation
5. **VSL or Demo to Booking Call**
6. **Proposal and Contract**
7. **Delivery of Photos/Videos**
8. **Review Request**
9. **Referral Request**

#### ManyChat Setup Using Trigger Words
- **ManyChat Trigger Word:** "See the magic behind your wedding day."
  - When users interact with this trigger, they are directed to a customized consultation call booking page.

#### Monetization Strategies
1. **Retainer Services:**
   - Monthly retainer for social media content creation and management.
2. **Stock Footage Sales:**
   - Sell stock footage of wedding preparations and events via subscription or per clip.
3. **Workshops/Training Programs:**
   - Offer workshops on wedding photography techniques and storytelling.

#### Weekly KPIs
1. Number of new followers gained each week.
2. Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares) for posts.
3. Conversion rate from social media to consultations.
4. Average booking value per client.
5. Referral rate from satisfied clients.

#### Objection Map
- **Objection:** "Other photographers offer lower prices."
  - **Response:** Emphasize the quality of storytelling and personalized service OTG provides, which justifies higher pricing.
- **Objection:** "I don't know what to expect with wedding photography."
  - **Response:** Offer a detailed consultation session to discuss their vision and needs, providing examples from previous projects.

#### What NOT to Do
1. Avoid posting generic or vague content that does not provide immediate value.
2. Do not focus solely on price-based competition; highlight unique selling points such as storytelling and behind-the-scenes access.
3. Avoid over-editing videos; keep them authentic and relatable.

#### Testing Plan
- Post 50-100 short-form videos across Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Stories, and LinkedIn.
- Identify top-performing hooks (visual, written, verbal) and formats based on engagement metrics.
- Double down on successful content patterns to maximize reach and conversions.
