### Growth Strategy for Wedding Photography and Videography

#### 5 Key Questions:
1. **WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?**
   - Exact Target Customer: Engaged couples planning their wedding day.
   - Online Presence: Primarily on Instagram and TikTok, looking for inspiration and advice.

2. **WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?**
   - Offer: Personalized destination wedding packages with unique travel content and practical tips.

3. **HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?**
   - Strategy: Leverage organic social media growth through Instagram Reels and TikTok Shorts, focusing on viral hooks and formats identified by the Viral Filter Agent.

4. **WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?**
   - Unique Selling Points: Personalized packages tailored to each couple's vision, unique travel content, and practical tips that resonate emotionally.

5. **WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?**
   - Moment: After showcasing value through emotional and practical content, trigger a call-to-action (CTA) with clear benefits such as stress-free planning and memorable moments captured in high resolution.

#### 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan:
1. **30-Day Goal:** Establish OTG's presence on Instagram and TikTok by posting at least one viral video per day using the top hooks (Practical Tips & Checklists, Emotional Moments & Highlights, Destination Weddings & Travel).
2. **60-Day Goal:** Increase engagement through interactive content like polls, Q&A sessions, and user-generated content challenges to build a community around OTG.
3. **90-Day Goal:** Scale up by collaborating with wedding planners, venues, and other influencers in the niche to reach new audiences and drive more qualified leads.

#### Complete Funnel - Content to Closed Deal:
1. **Social Content** (Instagram Reels, TikTok Shorts)
2. **Comment or DM Trigger** via ManyChat
3. **Landing Page**
4. **Lead Magnet Delivered** (e.g., wedding planning checklist PDF)
5. **VSL or Demo** showcasing OTG's services and unique selling points
6. **Booking Call** to discuss personalized packages
7. **Proposal** with detailed pricing and package options
8. **Contract Signing**
9. **Delivery of Services** (Photography & Videography)
10. **Review Request**
11. **Referral Request**

#### ManyChat Setup:
- **Trigger Word:** `📸`
- **ManyChat Trigger:** When a user comments or DMs with the trigger word `📸`, they are directed to a personalized landing page offering wedding planning tips and booking consultations.

#### 3 Monetization Strategies:
1. **Retainer Services:** Offer monthly retainer services for ongoing social media content creation, ensuring consistent engagement and visibility.
2. **Stock Footage Licensing:** Monetize the stock footage library created during destination weddings and travel projects.
3. **Referral Program:** Encourage clients to refer friends and family by offering a discount on future bookings or exclusive perks.

#### Weekly KPIs:
1. **Engagement Rate** (likes, comments, shares)
2. **Follower Growth**
3. **Website Traffic from Social Media**
4. **Lead Generation Rate**

#### What NOT to Do:
- Avoid posting generic wedding content without a clear value proposition.
- Don't rely solely on paid advertising; focus on organic growth first.

#### Testing Plan:
1. **Post 50-100 Videos:** Use the top hooks and formats identified by the Viral Filter Agent.
2. **Identify Winners:** Analyze engagement metrics to identify which videos resonate most with the target audience.
3. **Double Down on Successful Hooks & Formats:** Repurpose winning content into multiple variations and scale up organic reach.
