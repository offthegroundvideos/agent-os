### Growth Strategy for Wedding Videography/Photography

**1. WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?**
- **Exact Target Customer:** Couples aged 25-35 planning their weddings.
- **Online Presence:**
  - Instagram: For visual inspiration, engagement with wedding content creators.
  - TikTok: For quick tips and viral trends related to DIY wedding photography.

**2. WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?**
- **Primary Offer:** A personalized wedding videography/photography package tailored for unique moments during their special day.
- **Value Proposition:** High-quality, cinematic visuals with a personal touch to capture every significant moment.

**3. HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?**
- **Organic Channels:**
  - Instagram Reels and TikTok duets/challenges to showcase DIY photography tips and unique wedding moments.
  - Engaging captions and hashtags to increase visibility organically.
  
- **Low-Cost Paid Ads:**
  - Targeted Facebook ads with lookalike audiences of engaged couples.

**4. WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?**
- **Differentiation Points:**
  - Personalized service that aligns closely with the couple's vision and unique moments.
  - High-quality visuals and cinematic editing.
  - Additional value through educational content (DIY tips, unique moment capture).

**5. WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?**
- **Timing:** After showcasing a series of high-quality wedding videos on Instagram/TikTok.
- **Trigger Point:** Use ManyChat triggers to engage viewers who show interest in DIY photography or unique moments.

### 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan

**30-Day Goal:**
- Establish OTG as the go-to resource for wedding videography and photography.
- Post 50 high-quality videos showcasing unique moments and DIY tips on Instagram/TikTok.
- Engage with top wedding content creators to build credibility.

**60-Day Goal:**
- Increase organic reach by at least 25% through consistent posting and engagement.
- Launch a targeted Facebook ad campaign for lookalike audiences of engaged couples.
- Begin collecting leads via ManyChat triggers and direct messaging.

**90-Day Goal:**
- Double the number of followers on Instagram/TikTok compared to Day 30.
- Convert at least 15% of engaged viewers into qualified leads through ManyChat sequences.
- Showcase customer testimonials and success stories to build trust.

### Complete Funnel

**Content to Closed Deal Funnel:**
1. **Social Content:** High-quality videos showcasing unique moments and DIY tips.
2. **Trigger Engagement:** Use ManyChat triggers for viewers interested in DIY photography or unique moments.
3. **Landing Page:** Direct them to a landing page with more details about personalized wedding packages.
4. **Lead Magnet:** Offer a free consultation call to discuss their vision.
5. **VSL/Demo:** Share a video showcasing OTG's work and services.
6. **Booking Call:** Schedule a call to finalize the package.
7. **Proposal & Contract:** Provide a detailed proposal with a contract for signing.
8. **Delivery:** Capture and deliver high-quality wedding videos/photography.
9. **Review Request:** Ask for reviews after delivery.
10. **Referral Request:** Encourage satisfied clients to refer other couples.

### ManyChat Setup
- **Trigger Word:** "Learn More"
- **ManyChat Flow:**
  - Trigger on engagement with DIY photography or unique moment content.
  - Offer a free consultation call via a button in the chatbot.

### Monetization Strategies

1. **Lead Magnet & Consultation Calls:**
   - Free consultations to understand client needs and offer personalized packages.

2. **Stock Footage Licensing:**
   - Sell stock footage from wedding projects on OTG's website or third-party platforms like Shutterstock.

3. **Referral Program:**
   - Incentivize existing clients to refer new couples for a discount on future services.

### Weekly KPIs
- **Engagement Rate:** Increase by 5% weekly.
- **Follower Growth:** Aim for at least 10 new followers daily.
- **Lead Generation:** Convert at least 2 engaged viewers into leads per week.

### What NOT to Do

- Avoid posting generic wedding content without unique hooks or value.
- Do not over-rely on paid ads without building organic reach first.

### Testing Plan
- Post 50-100 videos across Instagram and TikTok, focusing on different hooks (DIY tips, dramatic drone footage).
- Use analytics to identify top-performing videos in terms of engagement and conversion rates.
- Double down on successful hooks and formats by creating more content aligned with these patterns.
