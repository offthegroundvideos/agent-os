### Growth Strategy for Wedding Photography and Videography

#### 1. WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?
**Target Customer:**
- **Primary Audience:** Couples planning their weddings.
- **Secondary Audience:** Event organizers, wedding planners, and real estate agents (for staging photos).

**Where They Spend Time Online:**
- Instagram and TikTok for visual inspiration and quick tips.
- LinkedIn for professional networking and high-quality portfolio showcases.

#### 2. WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?
**One Offer That Converts Fastest:**
- **Offer:** Custom wedding photography and videography packages with a focus on capturing special moments (e.g., first look, ceremony highlights, reception dance floor).
- **Value Proposition:** OTG Icon provides personalized services tailored to each couple's unique love story, ensuring every moment is captured beautifully.

#### 3. HOW do we get in front of them at the lowest cost?
**Low-Cost Methods:**
- Leverage Instagram and TikTok for organic reach through high-quality content.
- Engage with local wedding communities on social media platforms to build relationships.
- Collaborate with event organizers and planners for referrals.

#### 4. WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?
**Unique Selling Points:**
- **Expertise in Capturing Special Moments:** Detailed behind-the-scenes content showcasing expertise.
- **Personalization and Storytelling:** Tailored services that capture the essence of each couple's unique love story.
- **High-Quality Visuals:** Stunning visuals with professional-grade equipment and editing techniques.

#### 5. WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?
**Right Moment:**
- After showcasing a series of high-quality content (videos, reels, images) that highlights OTG Icon’s expertise.
- When potential clients express interest through comments or direct messages asking about services or packages.

### Growth Strategy Deliverables

#### 1. 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan with Specific Milestones
**30-Day Goal:**
- Publish a consistent series of high-quality wedding photography and videography content on Instagram and TikTok.
- Engage with the community by responding to comments and messages, building relationships.

**60-Day Goal:**
- Start collaborating with local event organizers and planners for referrals.
- Begin posting client testimonials and success stories on LinkedIn.

**90-Day Goal:**
- Launch a referral program encouraging current clients to refer new business.
- Offer exclusive discounts or packages for early bookings.

#### 2. Complete Funnel - Content to Closed Deal
**Funnel Steps:**
1. **Social Content:** Publish high-quality wedding content on Instagram and TikTok.
2. **Trigger to ManyChat:** Use trigger words (e.g., [BOOK_NOW]) in captions to direct users to a chatbot.
3. **Landing Page:** Direct users to a landing page with detailed service offerings.
4. **Lead Magnet Delivered:** Offer a free consultation or discount for booking early.
5. **VSL or Demo:** Provide a video sales letter (VSL) showcasing previous work and testimonials.
6. **Booking Call:** Schedule a call to discuss custom package options.
7. **Contract Delivery:** Send over the contract with detailed terms and payment plans.
8. **Delivery of Services:** Capture the wedding day moments and deliver high-quality photos and videos.
9. **Review Request:** Ask for feedback post-delivery.
10. **Referral Request:** Encourage referrals for future bookings.

#### 3. ManyChat Setup Using Trigger Words
**Trigger Word:**
- [BOOK_NOW]

**ManyChat Sequence:**
- Welcome message with a brief introduction and offer of a free consultation.
- Follow-up messages guiding users to the booking page or scheduling call options.

#### 4. 3 Monetization Strategies
1. **Retainer Services:** Offer monthly retainer services for ongoing content creation and social media management.
2. **Stock Footage Licensing:** Sell exclusive wedding footage clips through a stock library.
3. **Workshops and Masterclasses:** Host online workshops or masterclasses on wedding photography techniques.

#### 5. Weekly KPIs
- **Engagement Rate:** Measure likes, comments, shares per post.
- **Follower Growth Rate:** Track new followers gained weekly.
- **Conversion Rate:** Monitor the percentage of users who book services after engaging with content.

#### 6. What NOT to Do
- Avoid posting vague or generic content that does not add value immediately.
- Refrain from overpromising and underdelivering on service quality.
- Stay away from overwhelming clients with too many options, leading to decision paralysis.

#### 7. Testing Plan - How OTG Should Post 50-100 Videos, Identify Winners, and Double Down
**Testing Plan:**
- **Publish Phase:** Post at least 50 videos across Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- **Analyze Performance:** Track engagement rates, conversion metrics, and audience feedback.
- **Identify Winners:** Focus on content that garners the highest engagement and conversions.
- **Double Down:** Repurpose successful formats into multiple variations to maximize reach.
