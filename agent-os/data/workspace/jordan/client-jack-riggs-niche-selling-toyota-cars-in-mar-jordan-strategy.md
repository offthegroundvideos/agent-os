### Growth Strategy for Selling Toyota Cars in Marin County

#### 1. Target Customer and Online Presence:
- **Exact Target Customers:** Commuters and families living or working in Marin County, San Rafael, and the Bay Area.
- **Where They Spend Time Online:**
  - Instagram (Reels)
  - TikTok
  - Local community groups on Facebook

#### 2. Fastest Converting Offer:
- **Primary Offer:** Schedule a time to come in and test drive a Toyota car.

#### 3. Lowest-Cost Acquisition Method:
- Use local community groups, event sponsorships, and referral programs to reach the target audience.
- Leverage Instagram Reels and TikTok to showcase customer testimonials, product demonstrations, and interactive challenges.

#### 4. Why Choose OTG Over Competitors:
- **Differentiation:** Focus on creating engaging content that resonates with commuters and families through interactive challenges and customer testimonials.
- **Local Expertise:** Showcase the local community involvement and strong connections within Marin County.

#### 5. Right Moment to Ask for Sale:
- After showcasing value through engaging content, prompt viewers to schedule a test drive or visit the dealership directly.

### Growth Strategy Deliverables

#### Primary Offer
**Primary Offer:** Schedule a time to come in and test drive a Toyota car.

#### 30/60/90 Day Growth Plan
**30-Day Goal:**
- Post 5 customer testimonial videos on Instagram Reels.
- Publish 5 product demonstration skits on TikTok.
- Engage with local community groups via Facebook and host one event sponsorship.

**60-Day Goal:**
- Double the number of posts (10 each on Instagram Reels and TikTok).
- Expand engagement to include more local community groups and events.
- Begin testing referral programs to drive word-of-mouth marketing.

**90-Day Goal:**
- Triple the post frequency (15 each on Instagram Reels and TikTok).
- Optimize content based on performance data from previous posts.
- Secure additional event sponsorships and deepen partnerships with local influencers.

#### Complete Funnel
1. **Social Content:** Engaging videos showcasing customer testimonials, product demonstrations, and interactive challenges.
2. **Trigger to ManyChat:**
   - Instagram Reels: Use trigger words like "SCHEDULE_NOW" in comments to direct users to a chatbot.
   - TikTok: Include CTA buttons or swipe-up links directing viewers to book an appointment via ManyChat.
3. **Landing Page:** A simple landing page with a form asking for contact information and preferred test drive time.
4. **Lead Magnet Delivered:** Send a personalized email with the booking confirmation and additional Toyota car brochures.
5. **VSL or Demo:** Offer a virtual showroom tour via video call to further engage potential customers.
6. **Booking Call:** Schedule an in-person visit for a test drive.
7. **Proposal:** Provide detailed information about available models, financing options, and warranties.
8. **Contract:** Finalize the purchase agreement.
9. **Delivery & Review Request:**
   - Deliver the car and provide customer service support.
   - Send follow-up emails requesting reviews and referrals.

#### ManyChat Setup
- Use trigger words like "SCHEDULE_NOW" in comments on Instagram Reels to direct users to a chatbot.
- Include CTA buttons or swipe-up links directing viewers to book an appointment via ManyChat on TikTok.

#### Monetization Strategies
1. **Event Sponsorships:** Partner with local events and festivals to promote Toyota cars through branded content.
2. **Referral Programs:** Offer incentives for existing customers who refer friends and family to buy a car from OTG Icon.
3. **Stock Footage Licensing:** Sell footage of Marin County landscapes and Toyota cars to travel brands, media companies, and other businesses.

#### Weekly KPIs
- Number of new followers on Instagram and TikTok.
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares) per post.
- Number of scheduled test drives through ManyChat.
- Conversion rate from social posts to booked appointments.

#### Objection Map
1. **Objection:** "Too busy to visit the dealership."
   - Answer: Offer virtual showroom tours via video call and flexible scheduling options for in-person visits.

2. **Objection:** "Don't trust online dealerships."
   - Answer: Highlight local expertise, community involvement, and strong customer testimonials.

3. **Objection:** "More convenient to buy from a large dealership."
   - Answer: Emphasize personalized service, competitive pricing, and loyalty programs tailored for the local market.

#### What NOT to Do
- Avoid posting vague or disconnected content that does not directly relate to the offer.
- Do not rely solely on paid ads; focus on organic growth through engaging content and community engagement.
- Avoid overloading social media with too many posts without proper optimization and analysis of performance data.

#### Testing Plan
1. **Post 50-100 Videos:** Start by posting a mix of customer testimonials, product demonstrations, and interactive challenges across Instagram Reels and TikTok.
2. **Identify Winners:** Analyze the engagement rate, conversion rate, and follower growth for each type of content.
3. **Double Down on Successful Hooks & Formats:**
   - Continue to create more videos in successful formats while testing new angles and hooks.
