1. **WHO is the exact target customer and where do they spend time online?**
   - Exact Target Customer: Dog owners aged 25-45 who are seeking practical training advice for their pets.
   - Online Presence: Primarily on Instagram Reels and TikTok, where they engage with short-form content related to pet care and dog training.

2. **WHAT is the one offer that converts them fastest?**
   - Offer: A specialized online dog training course tailored to niche issues like anxiety, aggression, or specific breed challenges.
   
3. **HOW do we get in front of them at lowest cost?**
   - Strategy: Leverage organic social media growth by creating and sharing high-quality, engaging content on Instagram Reels and TikTok. Utilize relevant hashtags and community engagement.

4. **WHY will they choose OTG over competitors?**
   - Unique Selling Proposition (USP): OTG offers personalized training solutions with expert advice from seasoned trainers who specialize in addressing niche issues that other brands may overlook.
   
5. **WHEN is the right moment to ask for the sale?**
   - Timing: After providing value through several posts and establishing trust, direct viewers to a landing page where they can sign up for the specialized training course.

### Growth Strategy

#### 30/60/90 Day Plan
- **30 Days:** Establish a strong online presence by posting engaging content daily on Instagram Reels and TikTok. Focus on building an audience through organic reach.
- **60 Days:** Introduce interactive sessions and expert advice posts to engage the audience further. Start collecting email addresses for future targeted campaigns.
- **90 Days:** Launch a specialized training course offer, driving traffic from social media to a dedicated landing page.

#### Funnel Architecture
1. **Social Content (Instagram Reels & TikTok)**
   - Hook: Problem-solving tips and interactive training sessions.
2. **Trigger to ManyChat**
   - Trigger Words: "LEARN MORE", "JOIN NOW"
3. **Landing Page**
   - Offer: Specialized Online Dog Training Course
4. **Lead Magnet (Free Resource)**
   - Free eBook: Top 10 Tips for Effective Dog Training
5. **VSL or Demo**
   - Video showcasing the benefits of OTG's specialized training.
6. **Booking Call**
   - Consultation to tailor the course to specific needs.
7. **Contract & Delivery**
   - Enrollment in the training course with a personalized plan.

#### ManyChat Setup
- Trigger Words: "LEARN MORE", "JOIN NOW"
- Automated Welcome Message: Introduce the specialized training and offer a free eBook.
- Follow-Up Sequence: Send a series of messages guiding users to the landing page for enrollment.

#### Monetization Strategies
1. **Online Courses:** Offer specialized dog training courses with personalized coaching sessions.
2. **Consulting Services:** Provide one-on-one consulting services for complex training issues.
3. **Stock Footage Licensing:** Sell high-quality video clips from OTG’s productions to pet-related businesses and content creators.

#### Weekly KPIs
- Engagement Rate (Likes, Comments, Shares)
- Follower Growth Rate
- Click-through Rate on CTAs
- Email List Growth

#### What NOT to Do
- Avoid generic or vague content that does not provide immediate value.
- Refrain from pushing sales too aggressively without building trust first.

#### Testing Plan
1. **Initial Phase (Week 1-2):** Post 30 videos focusing on problem-solving tips and interactive sessions.
2. **Evaluation Phase (Week 3-4):** Identify the top-performing hooks and formats based on engagement metrics.
3. **Scaling Phase (After Week 5):** Double down on successful content themes and optimize for higher conversion rates.
