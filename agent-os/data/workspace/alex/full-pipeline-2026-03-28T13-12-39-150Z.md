### Market Overview and Opportunity Size
The luxury destination wedding photography market is a niche but lucrative segment of the wedding industry. The global wedding industry is estimated to be worth over $300 billion, with an increasing trend towards destination weddings due to travel becoming more accessible. Destination weddings can cost significantly more than traditional weddings, often ranging from $25,000 to over $1 million. For photographers and videographers specializing in this niche, the focus on luxury and premium services attracts clients willing to pay a higher price for high-quality visuals.

Key players include established brands like Grace Ormonville Photography and The Great Day Wedding Co., as well as emerging boutique studios that offer bespoke experiences tailored to destination weddings.
