USER: Give me a quick answer on strong offers.

ALEX: A strong offer in any niche, including dog training, has several key components:

1. **Clear Value Proposition**: Clearly state what problem your service solves.
2. **Immediate Relevance**: Address an immediate issue pet owners face (e.g., leash pulling).
3. **Practical Examples**: Provide concrete examples of how the solution works.
4. **Strong CTA**: Include a direct call-to-action, like signing up for a program or contacting you.
5. **Proof and Social Proof**: Use testimonials and case studies to build trust.

For dog training:
- **Quick Fixes**: Offer challenges with daily tips (e.g., "5-Day Challenge").
- **Comprehensive Programs**: Provide in-depth training programs (e.g., "30-Day Mastery Program").
