The photography niche in Marin County is highly competitive, with a mix of established and emerging photographers vying for attention from models seeking high-quality photoshoots. Key players include both local businesses and independent photographers who leverage social media platforms to showcase their work and attract clients.

- **Instagram:** Dominated by visual storytelling through Reels and Stories, emphasizing curated aesthetics and behind-the-scenes content.
- **TikTok:** Focused on short-form video tutorials, quick styling tips, and direct interaction with models through duets and challenges.
- **Facebook:** More traditional in approach, featuring longer posts about portfolios, testimonials, and client success stories.
- **Threads:** Emerging as a platform for detailed discussions and Q&A sessions, allowing photographers to engage deeply with their audience.

### TARGET AUDIENCE

The target audience for a photography business in Marin County includes young adult models aged 18-30, primarily women, who are active on social media platforms like Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and Threads. They seek high-quality photoshoots to build their portfolios or personal brands.

### OTG OPPORTUNITY

OTG can own the space by focusing on personalized storytelling techniques in photoshoots that set them apart from competitors. This includes offering unique post-production effects and exclusive client perks such as social media teasers, which cater specifically to models looking for a distinctive edge.

### LEAD SOURCES

- **Social Media:** Direct engagement through Instagram Reels, TikTok challenges, Facebook posts, and Threads discussions.
- **Networking Events:** Local photography events and workshops where models can meet photographers in person.
- **Online Communities:** Joining online forums and groups dedicated to modeling and photography.
