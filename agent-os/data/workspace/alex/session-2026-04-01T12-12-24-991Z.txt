ALEX: ### WHAT MAKES A STRONG OFFER

A strong offer in the dog training niche is one that:
1. **Addresses Specific Problems:** Clearly identifies common issues pet owners face (e.g., excessive barking, leash pulling).
2. **Provides Immediate Value:** Offers practical solutions with quick wins.
3. **Includes Proven Methods:** Uses evidence-based techniques backed by professional expertise.
4. **Has Clear Deliverables:** Specifies what the client will receive (e.g., training sessions, videos, guides).
5. **Builds Trust:** Includes testimonials or case studies from satisfied clients.
6. **Offers a Unique Angle:** Differentiates itself from competitors through niche-specific focus.
