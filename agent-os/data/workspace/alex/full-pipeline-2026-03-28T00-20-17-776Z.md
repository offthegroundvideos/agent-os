## Jordan

**Wedding Photography Business Promotion Strategy**

---

**1. 30/60/90 Day Action Plan:**

- **Days 1-7 (Week 1):**
  - Set up and optimize social media accounts across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Facebook.
  - Develop a detailed content calendar for the next 90 days.

- **Weeks 2-4 (Days 8-30):**
  - Begin daily posting on Instagram and TikTok with engaging visual and verbal hooks.
  - Start engaging with followers through comments and DMs.
  - Launch initial posts on LinkedIn and Facebook, focusing on educational content and community building.

- **Months 2-3 (Days 31-60):**
  - Analyze performance data to optimize posting schedules and content strategy.
  - Refine engagement tactics based on analytics insights.
  - Expand content creation to other platforms as identified by performance metrics.

- **Months 4-6 (Days 61-90):**
  - Introduce monetization strategies such as VSLs for photography packages.
  - Launch digital product sales and affiliate marketing efforts.
  - Finalize partnership collaborations with local businesses and influencers.

---

**2. Monetization Opportunities:**

- **Primary Revenue Stream:** Offer wedding photography packages through detailed Value Selling Letters (VSLs) targeting different customer segments (e.g., basic, premium, luxury).
- **Secondary Revenue Stream:** Sell digital products like eBooks on storytelling techniques in wedding photography or online workshops.
- **Tertiary Revenue Stream:** Engage in affiliate marketing with wedding vendors to promote related services and earn commissions.

---

**3. Partnership Ideas:**

- **Collaborations:** Partner with makeup artists, wedding planners, and florists for co-branded content and joint promotions.
- **Local Business Partnerships:** Collaborate with local event venues or hotels for cross-promotion during weddings.
- **Influencer Shoutouts:** Work with influencers in the wedding space to reach a broader audience through sponsored posts.

---

**4. KPIs to Track:**

- **Engagement Metrics:** Monitor likes, comments, shares, and DM interactions across all platforms.
- **Follower Growth:** Track daily and monthly follower growth rates to assess platform effectiveness.
- **Conversion Rates:** Measure the percentage of social media engagement that converts into leads or sales.
- **Revenue Tracking:** Regularly evaluate revenue generated from monetization strategies against set targets.
